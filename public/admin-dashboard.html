How Auto-Optimization Works

On the 1st of every month at 2:00 AM, our AI automatically searches for current rental prices for Studio/1BR · 28–42 sqm · Fully Furnished · CCTV · Fiber Optic units in Calamba and Nuvali/Santa Rosa. It then factors in your occupancy rate and inquiry demand to compute the optimal price — and applies it automatically. You can still override any price at any time using the card buttons below.
